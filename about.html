Since November 1871

Family-owned. Two plants. One metal shop.

Sioux City Foundry Co. began on the corner of Main and Seventh streets in Sioux City, Iowa. Today the company runs Plant 1 in Sioux City and Plant 2 in South Sioux City, Nebraska, as a One-Stop Metal Shop for warehouse steel, rebar, contract manufacturing, and iron castings.
