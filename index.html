From Main & Seventh to two modern plants

Sioux City Foundry Co. started in November 1871 in Sioux City, Iowa. Today the company operates Plant 1 in Sioux City and Plant 2 in South Sioux City, Nebraska, led by Andy Galinsky, CEO, and Adam Galinsky, President.
