Rebar

Black and epoxy-coated reinforcing steel for construction projects. Cut, bend, and coat in-house so schedules stay on the floor that owns the material.

Contract Manufacturing

Welding, machining & tanks

Contract manufacturing covers welding, the machining center, beam coping, and Sherman Tank fabricated tanks. Work is built to customer specifications.

  • Production and custom welding
  • Machining center: drill, bore, mill, cope, and thread mill
  • Beam coping
  • Sherman Tank fabricated tanks (build-to-spec)

Tank work is described as fabricated to customer requirements. This sample site does not claim ASME, DOT, or API certification for tanks.
